Privacy note

VANTA collects your name, email, optional phone number, selected service, issue description, and your authorization and privacy confirmations. These details are used only to review and respond to your service request.

Requests are stored in a private database on the server hosting this site. Only the site operator should have access. Do not submit passwords, login codes, recovery codes, payment details, or sensitive personal data. For account recovery, use the platform’s official process.

Set a retention period and a contact email before publishing. The operator should remove requests when they are no longer needed and provide a way for clients to request access or deletion. This draft has no public admin page and no direct marketing.
